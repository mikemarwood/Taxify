Questions people ask before they sign up.

Is Taxify actually Australian?

Yes. It is built and supported here by Mikes App Hub, priced in Australian dollars, and it runs on the Australian financial year — 1 July to 30 June, not a US one.

Do I need to be good with computers?

If you can take a photograph on your phone, you can use Taxify. Snap the receipt and it is stored, dated and put in a category for you.

What happens when the 14 days are up?

Nothing automatic. There is no card on the trial, so there is nothing to charge. You pick a plan only if you want to keep going.

Will my accountant be able to use it?

That is what the export is for: one file holding your spreadsheet, the PDFs and every original receipt, ready to hand straight over.

Can I keep personal and business separate?

Yes, and rental properties too. Each one keeps its own expenses and its own totals, so nothing has to be untangled at the end of the year.

Is there an app for my phone?

Taxify runs in any phone browser with nothing to install, and there is an Android app to download. On an iPhone, add it to your home screen from Safari and it opens like an app.
